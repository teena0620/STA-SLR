R²

Share of the ups and downs in Y that the line explains. 1 = perfect, 0 = no better than the average.

MAE

Average size of the prediction mistakes, in the same units as Y.

RMSE

Like MAE but big mistakes count extra. It is always ≥ MAE.

Careful!

Regression shows association, not causation . Metrics here are computed on the same data used to fit, so they do not prove future accuracy.

Methodology

1. Data collection

Order-level records: date, product, category, quantity, price, revenue, advertising spend.

2. Cleaning & preprocessing

Parse dates and numbers, drop invalid X/Y pairs from the model (and report how many), derive revenue = quantity × price when needed.

3. Exploratory analysis

Mean, median, spread, missing values and column types reveal data quality and shape.

4. Trend visualization

Monthly revenue, category and product rankings, distribution and scatter plots.

5. Simple Linear Regression

b₁ = Σ(x−x̄)(y−ȳ) / Σ(x−x̄)², b₀ = ȳ − b₁x̄ (least squares).

6. Model evaluation

R², MAE, RMSE and residual plots check fit and spot patterns the line misses.

7. Business interpretation

Translate slope and trends into plain advice, noting limits and non-causality.
